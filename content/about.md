I am a programmer currently employed as a Senior Software Engineer working at the intersection of performance
engineering and data infrastructure, based in Canada. Outside my work I like to contribute to opensource; I currently
maintain [tpchgen-rs](https://github.com/clflushopt/tpchgen-rs) the [fastest](https://datafusion.apache.org/blog/2025/04/10/fastest-tpch-generator/)
TPC-H & TPC-DS generator out there and I contribute to [Apache Datafusion](https://github.com/apache/datafusion/issues).

Outside of that I spend a lot of time on experimental and research projects from [SIGMOD contest problems](https://github.com/clflushopt/sigmod-2025),
investigating [how CPUs behave](https://github.com/clflushopt/uarch-xyz) and [all](https://github.com/clflushopt/llvm-riscv-lifter)
[sorts](https://github.com/clflushopt/coldbrew) of [compilers](https://github.com/clflushopt/icepop).


In a previous life I worked on implementing cryptography libraries and numerical solvers in C++.
